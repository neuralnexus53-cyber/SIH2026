About Us

Government e Marketplace (GeM) is the National Public Procurement Portal of India. It is an end-to-end online marketplace for Central and State Government Ministries, Departments, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), and local bodies to procure common-use goods and services efficiently, transparently, and inclusively.

GeM 2.0 Portal

GeM 2.0 represents the next generation of public procurement. Built on modern cloud technologies, GeM 2.0 integrates artificial intelligence, advanced machine learning matchmaking, a unified search engine, and predictive analytics to make procurement transparent, robust, and cost-effective.

Our Initiatives

From supporting local micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) through targeted training, to fostering innovative start-ups via the Start-up Runway, we drive sustainable development and make government public procurement highly inclusive for every seller in the country.

Subscription Model

Our flexible subscription model ensures fair access for sellers of all scales. Micro and small enterprises enjoy zero annual maintenance fees, while larger corporations subscribe to highly optimized tiers aligned with transaction volumes and advanced analytics support.
