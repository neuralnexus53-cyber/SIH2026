About Us

Government e Marketplace (GeM) is the National Public Procurement Portal of India. It is an end-to-end online marketplace for Central and State Government Ministries, Departments, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), and local bodies to procure common-use goods and services efficiently, transparently, and inclusively.

GeM 2.0 Portal

GeM 2.0 represents the next generation of public procurement. Built on modern web technologies, GeM 2.0 integrates artificial intelligence, advanced machine learning matchmaking, a unified search system, and predictive analytics to make procurement even more transparent, robust, and cost-effective.
